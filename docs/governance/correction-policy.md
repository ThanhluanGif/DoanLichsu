# Correction, moderation và changelog policy v1.0

Status: `DRAFT_PENDING_COUNCIL_SIGNOFF`  
Owner: Editorial Operations  
Last reviewed: 2026-08-13

## Intake

Anyone may report a factual, source, translation, accessibility, safety or rights issue
without creating a public account. Capture the minimum contact data, content ID, claim,
evidence/locator, urgency and consent. Never publish a reporter's private details.

## Service levels

Rights and safety reports are triaged within one business day; other valid reports within
three business days. Acknowledge, assign an owner, preserve the old version, investigate,
then publish a correction or reasoned closure. Critical harm/rights reports can hide the
item pending review.

## Moderation states

`RECEIVED`, `TRIAGED`, `IN_REVIEW`, `NEEDS_COUNCIL`, `CORRECTED`, `DECLINED`, `ARCHIVED`.
Every transition has actor, timestamp, reason and related diff. The public changelog
shows meaningful changes without exposing reporter identity or private notes.
