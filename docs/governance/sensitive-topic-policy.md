# Sensitive Topic Policy v1.0

Status: `DRAFT_PENDING_COUNCIL_SIGNOFF`  
Owner: Chief Historian (pending appointment)  
Last reviewed: 2026-08-13

## Scope

History of the Party and state, wars, sovereignty, borders, political figures,
international relations, identity, religion/ethnicity, living persons and claims likely
to cause harm or legal exposure are sensitive. The registry must classify a topic before
publication; classification can be escalated by any reviewer.

## Required review

Sensitive content needs two independent reviewers: (1) historian/source reviewer checks
chronology, provenance, locator and uncertainty; (2) curriculum/policy/language reviewer
checks programme alignment, wording, translation and non-incitement. A disagreement is
escalated to the Council with both reasoned positions. AI may summarize approved material
but may never decide the disagreement or publish a sensitive item (AI may never arbitrate
or override the dual-review decision).

## Writing rules

Use precise actors, dates and scope. Distinguish documented fact from interpretation and
memory. State what remains contested. Avoid slurs, collective blame, triumphalist
fabrication, unlabelled maps, anachronism and “all historians agree” without a real
survey. Use peaceful dispute settlement and international-law terminology where relevant.

## Decision record

Each decision has topic ID, risk tier, reviewers, sources, open questions, disposition,
conditions, signed decision or explicit `PENDING_COUNCIL_SIGNOFF`, date and expiry.
Public UI must disclose when a page has internal verification only.
