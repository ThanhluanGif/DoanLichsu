# Quân Sử Việt — Editorial Charter v1.0

Status: `DRAFT_PENDING_COUNCIL_SIGNOFF`  
Owner: Content Lead  
Last reviewed: 2026-08-13

## Purpose

Quân Sử Việt is a bilingual public learning archive. It helps Vietnamese learners
understand history through primary records, accountable interpretation and respectful
questions. It does not replace a school textbook, lecturer, museum or original archive.

## Non-negotiable editorial principles

1. Separate fact, primary evidence, accepted interpretation and open debate.
2. Every public claim has a source, locator, access date, provenance and reviewer.
3. A discovery source is not automatically an authority for a sensitive claim.
4. No fabricated quotation, consensus, council approval, archive access or AI citation.
5. Vietnamese and English text must preserve scope, uncertainty and proper nouns.
6. Corrections are traceable: reason, diff, decision-maker, date and public changelog.
7. Learners are treated with dignity; no incitement, dehumanisation or partisan targeting.

## Publication states

`DRAFT` → `NEEDS_REVIEW` → `VERIFIED` → `PUBLISHED`, or `REJECTED`/`ARCHIVED`.
`VERIFIED` means an identified reviewer checked the evidence and process; it never
means an independent historian council has approved the item. Sensitive items require
two independent reviews before publication.

## Separation of duties

No person may be the sole author, reviewer and publisher of sensitive content. Legal/
rights can block media; Privacy/Security can block AI; Tech Lead can block an unsafe
release; Product Owner decides scope and kill/continue decisions.

## Release gate

The Product Owner may request release only after the Council records a signed decision,
or explicitly records a time-boxed pilot exception with scope, expiry and named owner.
Until then, this charter remains a draft and the public product must not imply council
endorsement.
