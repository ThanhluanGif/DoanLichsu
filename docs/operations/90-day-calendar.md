## Incident rota

Primary SRE/QA, secondary Tech Lead, content incident owner Content Lead, rights owner
Archivist/Rights, privacy/safety owner Security/Privacy. A real rota requires named
people, contact channels, on-call hours, budget and escalation acknowledgement; those are
not invented by this file.

## Pilot protocol

Invite 8 students, 4 university students, 4 teachers, 2 lecturers and 2 librarians only
after privacy review. No account required; obtain consent/guardian process where needed;
collect only task completion, comprehension delta, correction usefulness and qualitative
feedback. Compare AI-disabled baseline with internal-alpha tutor. Do not publish pilot
claims until sample, dates, consent and anonymised raw evidence are archived.

## Release blockers
