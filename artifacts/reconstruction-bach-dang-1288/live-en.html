Bạch Đằng 1288: reading a river battle

An educational reconstruction organised by phases, sources, and assumptions — not an exact restoration.

Overall confidence: MEDIUMA sourced interpretation, not an exact restoration of the past.

Interpretive diagram

Narrative fallback active

In 1288, Trần forces organised defence in the Bạch Đằng river environment. This reconstruction divides the account into three phases: preparing the terrain, a withdrawing fleet following the tide, and a counterattack as conditions changed. Arrows are educational locators; the full narrative, sources, and assumptions remain available without WebGL.

Read in sequence

Three narrative phases

  1. Before the battle

    Defenders used the river environment, stake field, and tide timing as coordinated conditions. Specific placement is simplified so a hypothesis is not presented as settled fact.

    Assumptions: The stake-field position in the diagram is an approximate locator.

    • Organise defence (defenders, MEDIUM)

  2. While the water is high

    The fleet withdraws into the river while conditions remain favourable. The arrow marks narrative direction, not speed, force size, or formation.

    Assumptions: Movement direction is abstracted from common accounts; it is not a complete battle map.

    • Withdraw into the river (fleet, LOW)

  3. The tide ebbs

    As the tide ebbs, the stake field and river forces create difficult conditions for the withdrawing fleet. The reconstruction does not simulate collisions, casualties, or outcomes through physics.

    Assumptions: The relationship among tide, stakes, and outcome is presented as a sourced interpretation.

    • Counterattack (defenders, MEDIUM)

Sources and limits

Paths on the diagram are broad interpretations, not GPS data or physics simulation. Tide timing and stake-field location follow the source narrative; uncertain details are labelled with confidence.
