## Scope

Strengthen the read-only/ledger generator for the 500-question AI golden set.
Every row must carry explicit reviewer role, authority scope and evidence
reference fields for each of two independent reviewers. The validator must
reject same-person dual approval, approval without identity/role/authority/
timestamp/evidence, invalid roles, malformed timestamps, and unresolved verdict
conflicts. Existing blank rows remain `PENDING_HUMAN_REVIEW`; this card must not
invent reviewers, signatures or Council decisions.
