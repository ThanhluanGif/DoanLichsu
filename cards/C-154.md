## Scope

Repair the Wikimedia batch importer so a missing/nonexistent Commons title is not
turned into a fake `wikimedia-undefined` record. The importer must skip pages that
do not have a page identity/image payload, continue through additional bounded
search candidates until it collects exactly 300 valid metadata records, and expose
the skipped count. Re-run the checked-in batch and rights ledger so the pilot has
300 records with zero malformed metadata while every row remains `LINK_ONLY`,
`PENDING_REVIEW`, and binary serving stays disabled. This card does not approve
rights, invent reviewers, or claim partner permission.
