# C-158 — Fail-closed validator cho Wikimedia/partner rights handoff

status: done
deps: C-094, C-154
implements: infra

## Scope

Create a deterministic rights/partner handoff validator for the external
`partner-rights` gate. It must bind the packet to the 300-row Wikimedia batch
and rights ledger hashes, require zero invalid metadata, verify every selected
row is `LINK_ONLY` with `serveBinary=false`, and require two partner collection
records with owner/authority/timestamp/artifact hash. The validator must reject
binary-serving claims, path traversal, PII/secrets and self-approved rights.
A structurally complete link-only packet may pass schema, but
`officialPartnerRights=false`, `approvedForBinary=0`, `releaseAllowed=false`
and `publicBeta=false` must remain immutable.
