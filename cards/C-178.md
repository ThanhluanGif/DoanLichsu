section for every one of the 11 external release gates. Read the existing handoff packet without

keep the section fail-closed when owners/evidence are null. Use plain Vietnamese/English labels;
do not expose raw JSON or claim any approval. Preserve the existing release blocker list and
`publicBeta=false` behavior.
