# C-180 — Generate an external evidence submission kit

status: done
deps: C-179
implements: infra

## Scope

Generate a bilingual, machine-readable submission kit from the canonical
external evidence ledger, then rebind the derived local release/readiness
artifacts to the final source snapshot. The kit must list all 11 gates, their
required owner role, required evidence and next action, plus the exact fields
an authorised owner must provide (`owner`, `authority`, `verifiedAt`, `artifact`,
`sha256`, and an optional note). It must remain a blank handoff template: all
gates stay `PENDING`, `publicBeta=false`, `releaseAllowed=false`, and no
secret, reviewer, signature or approval is invented. This reduces submission
errors; it does not change the canonical ledger or grant external approval.
