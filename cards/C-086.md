## Scope

Update `production-readiness` to consume current local evidence artifacts (quality,
backup/restore, uptime observation, performance observation, AI eval and security
pack) and produce explicit `PASS_LOCAL_ONLY`/`BLOCKED_EXTERNAL` statuses. It must
never report official production readiness or Public Beta readiness from local,
disposable or quick-tunnel evidence.
